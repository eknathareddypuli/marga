Watch out for

Warning

Assume any exposed secret is copied, so revoke first and clean history after.

Warning

Base64 is not encryption, so enable encryption at rest and limit who can read Secrets.

Warning

Probe the live endpoint, because the certificate in a store may not be the one served.

Warning

A federation trust with a wildcard subject lets any repository assume the role.
