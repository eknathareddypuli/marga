Watch out for

Warning

The default Delete policy on a composition can remove a production database with its namespace.

Warning

An ApplicationSet with automated sync on every cluster spreads a bad change in minutes, so use rings.

Warning

Backstage entities with no valid owner destroy trust in the portal.

Warning

Preview environments with no time to live become permanent cost.
