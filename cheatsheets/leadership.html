"STAR answers, decision records and feedback shapes."

In the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

STAR answer shape

Part Content
Situation The context in two sentences
Task What you were responsible for
Action What you did, using I not we
Result A measured outcome and a lesson

Decision record

Section Question
Context What problem and constraints?
Options What did we consider?
Decision What did we choose?
Consequences What gets easier and harder?

Feedback

Step Example
Behaviour In the review on Tuesday you interrupted twice
Impact The team stopped sharing ideas
Request Please let others finish

Watch out for

Warning

Do not skip the written proposal, because meetings forget and writing remains.

Warning

Praise in public and give hard feedback in private, with specific examples.

Warning

Never present a number without its source and its uncertainty.

Warning

A decision without an owner and a date is only a discussion.
