## STAR answer shape

| Part | Content |
|------|---------|
| Situation | The context in two sentences |
| Task | What you were responsible for |
| Action | What you did, using I not we |
| Result | A measured outcome and a lesson |

## Decision record

| Section | Question |
|---------|----------|
| Context | What problem and constraints? |
| Options | What did we consider? |
| Decision | What did we choose? |
| Consequences | What gets easier and harder? |

## Feedback

| Step | Example |
|------|---------|
| Behaviour | In the review on Tuesday you interrupted twice |
| Impact | The team stopped sharing ideas |
| Request | Please let others finish |
