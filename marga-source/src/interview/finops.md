## What is FinOps?
level: junior
FinOps is the practice of making cloud spend visible, owned and optimised together by engineering, finance and product. The phases are inform, optimize and operate, and the main number is cost per unit of business value, not only the total bill.

## How do you allocate shared costs?
level: mid
I publish a simple rule, such as splitting in proportion to direct spend or usage, so teams understand and trust it. Account structure gives reliable ownership, tags refine it, and shared platform cost is shown separately so that nobody argues about it.

## How do you find savings safely?
level: mid
I look for idle and orphaned resources and rightsizing candidates using percentiles over a representative period, then tag, notify the owner, stop or snapshot first and only then delete. Every action is recorded so that a mistake can be reversed.

## How do commitments and spot fit together?
level: senior
Commitments cover the steady baseline and flexible plans cover usage that may change, bought in steps. Spot handles stateless and restartable work with diversified types and interruption handling. A monthly review checks coverage and utilisation.

## Why does data transfer surprise teams?
level: mid
It is billed per gigabyte and hidden in architecture. Cross zone and cross region traffic, NAT gateway processing and egress add up, so I check flow logs for big talkers, use private endpoints and keep chatty services together where reliability allows.

## How do you keep savings from fading?
level: senior
I use budgets with owners, daily anomaly alerts, forecasts built from usage drivers, showback to teams and cost data next to latency in dashboards and design reviews. I avoid blame so that teams share data openly.
