## How do you choose an SLI?
level: mid
I ask what a user would call broken, then define good events over valid events and measure close to the user. For request services that is availability and latency, for pipelines freshness and correctness.

## How do you run an SLO workshop?
level: mid
I bring the product owner and engineers, list critical journeys, define what broken means, check data exists, choose targets from history and need, and write a signed error budget policy with owners and a review date.

## How do you cut alert noise?
level: mid
I measure pages by alert, classify each as page, ticket or delete, page on user impact such as burn rate, link runbooks and track pages per shift. Fewer meaningful pages protect the on call team.

## How do you run a game day?
level: senior
I write a specific scenario with injects, assign facilitator, scribe and observers, agree scope and an abort rule, let responders work without rescue and finish with findings that have owners and dates.

## What reliability tests belong in a pipeline?
level: senior
Load tests with realistic traffic, soak tests for leaks, fault injection for dependencies in staging and small production experiments, and canary gates that compare SLO signals with the baseline.

## How do you keep reliability funded?
level: senior
I keep a ranked risk register, reserve a share of each quarter, report a one page view to leaders and show results such as fewer repeat incidents and faster recovery.
